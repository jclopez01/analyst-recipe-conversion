Butter Baste

Makes ~1 cup

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed (Bastes, Brines & Sauces Excerpts).

Ingredients

  • 1/2 pound (2 sticks) unsalted butter, cut into chunks
  • 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 5 garlic cloves, crushed and peeled
  • 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes

Instructions

  1. Combine all ingredients in a medium saucepan over medium heat and melt the butter.
  2. Bring just to a gentle simmer and cook 2–4 minutes.
  3. Let stand at least 1 hour for flavors to infuse; reheat gently before using.
