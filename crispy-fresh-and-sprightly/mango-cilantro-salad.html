Mango Cilantro Salad

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed.

Ingredients

  • ½ cup freshly squeezed lime juice
  • 2 tablespoons Greek yogurt
  • ¼ cup mild olive oil
  • 2 cups julienned unripe mango (about 1 large mango)
  • 1 cup julienned ripe mango
  • 1 cup julienned cucumber
  • ½ cup thinly sliced red onion
  • ¼ cup thinly sliced (on the bias) scallions
  • ½ cup finely chopped fresh cilantro (including some stems)
  • 2 tablespoons thinly slivered (chiffonade) fresh mint leaves

Instructions

  1. For the dressing, combine all the ingredients in a small bowl, mixing well.
  2. Combine the mangoes, cucumber, red onion, scallions, cilantro, and mint in a large bowl, tossing well. Drizzle the dressing over the salad, tossing gently, and serve.
