Pickled Ramps

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ramps, green tops removed
  • 1 cup white wine vinegar
  • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tablespoon thinly sliced fresh ginger
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed and peeled
  • 1 tablespoon coriander seeds
  • 1½ teaspoons fennel seeds
  • 1 teaspoon black peppercorns

Instructions

  1. If necessary, split larger ramps lengthwise in half so they are all about the same thickness. Blanch the ramps in boiling salted water for 10 to 15 seconds, then immediately transfer to a bowl of ice water to cool. Drain and pat lightly dry with paper towels.
  2. Put the ramps in a canning jar that holds them loosely, and set aside.
  3. Combine the vinegar, sugar, ginger, garlic, chile pepper, bay leaf, coriander seeds, fennel seeds, and peppercorns in a medium saucepan and bring to a simmer, stirring to dissolve the sugar.
  4. Pour the brine over the ramps and seal the jar. Allow to cool, then refrigerate for at least 12 hours before serving. The ramps will keep for at least 5 days in the refrigerator.
