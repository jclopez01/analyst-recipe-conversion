Peach and Nectarine Salad with Slivered Almonds

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped red onion
  • 2 cups tender dandelion greens or arugula leaves
  • ½ cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • 2 tablespoons freshly squeezed lime juice
  • 1 tablespoon mild olive oil
  • ¼ cup toasted slivered almonds

Instructions

  1. Spread the sugar on a plate and coat the cut sides of the peaches with the sugar.
  2. Heat a flat cast-iron griddle or large cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat until hot. Add the butter and let it melt. Add the peaches, cut side down, and cook until well caramelized on the cut surfaces, 4 to 5 minutes. Transfer to a plate.
  3. Combine the nectarines, red onion, dandelion greens, and cilantro in a medium bowl. Add the lime juice, mild olive oil, and salt and pepper to taste and toss gently.
  4. Mound the nectarine salad in the center of a platter and arrange the caramelized peaches, cut side up, around it. Drizzle with balsamic vinegar and extra virgin olive oil, sprinkle with the toasted almonds, and serve.
