Classic Southern Baste

Makes ~3 cups (with acid component)

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed (Bastes, Brines & Sauces Excerpts).

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups extra-virgin olive oil
  • 10 tablespoons (1¼ sticks) unsalted butter
  • 1/2 cup rendered fat from the meat being cooked (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons grated Spanish onion
  • 2 tablespoons grated garlic (Microplane or mashed to a paste)
  • 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 tablespoon dark brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 teaspoons sea or kosher salt
  • 2 teaspoons freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • ACID COMPONENT (optional): 1/2 cup cider vinegar
  • ACID COMPONENT (optional): 1 tablespoon ketchup
  • ACID COMPONENT (optional): 1 tablespoon yellow mustard

Instructions

  1. Combine all fat-baste ingredients in a 2-quart saucepan; bring just to a simmer and remove from heat.
  2. Chill 1–2 days for best flavor; rewarm gently before using.
  3. Whisk in acid component just before using (or add later). Apply with an herb brush during cooking.
