Thyme, Rosemary, and Sage Salt

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed.

Ingredients

  • Makes approximately 1 cup
  • 1 cup sea or kosher salt
  • 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 tablespoon fresh rosemary leaves
  • 1 tablespoon thinly slivered (chiffonade) fresh sage leaves
  • 1 teaspoon ground fennel
  • ½ teaspoon ground juniper berries

Instructions

  1. Combine all the ingredients in a bowl and mash them with your fingers to mix the herbs in with the salt.
  2. Spread the mixture out in a thin, even layer on a parchment-lined dehydrator tray and dry in a dehydrater at 105°F for 12 hours. Alternatively, spread the mixture on a parchment-lined baking sheet, put in a convection oven at the lowest setting, prop the door ajar with the handle of a wooden spoon, and let dry completely, about 12 hours.
  3. Finely grind in a spice grinder or clean coffee grinder and dry for another 2 hours.
  4. Store in an airtight container at room temperature for up to 1 month.
