Very Basic Brine

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed.

Ingredients

  • Makes approximately 5 cups
  • ¼ cup sea or kosher salt
  • 3 cups water
  • 2 cups apple juice
  • 10 garlic cloves, crushed and peeled
  • 1 tablespoon coarsely ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Combine all the ingredients in a medium saucepan and bring to a boil over high heat. Transfer to a bowl or other container and allow to cool, then refrigerate overnight before using.
