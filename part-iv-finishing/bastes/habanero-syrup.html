Habanero Syrup

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed.

Ingredients

  • Makes approximately 1½ cups
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • ½ cup water
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed and peeled
  • ¼ cup finely chopped scallions

Instructions

  1. Combine the sugar, water, habanero, and garlic in a small saucepan and bring to a simmer, stirring to dissolve the sugar. Remove from the heat and stir in the scallions and lime juice. Let stand for at least 1 hour before using to bring out the flavors. The syrup can be refrigerated in a covered container for up to 1 week.
