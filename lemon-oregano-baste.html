Lemon Oregano Baste

Makes ~4 cups (with acid component)

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed (Bastes, Brines & Sauces Excerpts).

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups extra-virgin olive oil
  • 10 tablespoons (1¼ sticks) unsalted butter
  • 1/2 cup rendered fat from the meat being cooked (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons dried oregano
  • 2 tablespoons grated Spanish onion (Microplane)
  • 2 tablespoons grated red bell pepper (Microplane)
  • 1/2 cup grated celery (Microplane)
  • 2 tablespoons grated garlic (Microplane or mashed to a paste)
  • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
  • 2 teaspoons sea or kosher salt
  • 2 teaspoons freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • ACID COMPONENT (optional): 1/2 cup freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • ACID COMPONENT (optional): 1/2 cup white wine vinegar

Instructions

  1. Combine all fat-baste ingredients in a 2-quart saucepan; bring just to a simmer, then remove from heat.
  2. Chill 1–2 days for best flavor; rewarm gently before using.
  3. Whisk in acid component just before using, or add later as desired. Apply with an herb brush during cooking.
