Basic Baste

Makes ~4 cups (with acid component)

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed (Bastes, Brines & Sauces Excerpts).

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 10 tablespoons (1¼ sticks) unsalted butter
  • 1/2 cup rendered fat from the meat being cooked (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons grated Spanish onion
  • 2 tablespoons grated garlic (Microplane or mashed to a paste)
  • 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
  • 2 teaspoons sea or kosher salt
  • 2 teaspoons freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • ACID COMPONENT (optional): 1/4 cup freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • ACID COMPONENT (optional): 1/4 cup white wine vinegar

Instructions

  1. Combine all fat-baste ingredients in a 2-quart saucepan and bring just to a simmer; remove from heat.
  2. For best flavor, refrigerate 1–2 days in a sealed container. Rewarm gently to melt butter before using.
  3. If using the acid component, whisk into the fat baste just before using (or add later as specified in a recipe).
  4. Apply with an herb brush while cooking; avoid over-applying so the crust doesn’t get soggy.
