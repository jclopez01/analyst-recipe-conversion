Very Basic Brine

Makes ~5 cups

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed (Bastes, Brines & Sauces Excerpts).

Ingredients

  • 3 cups water
  • 2 cups apple juice
  • 1/4 cup sea or kosher salt
  • 10 garlic cloves, crushed and peeled
  • 1 tablespoon coarsely ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Combine all ingredients in a medium saucepan; bring to a boil over high heat.
  2. Transfer to a container and let cool; refrigerate overnight before using.
