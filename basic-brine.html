Basic Brine

Makes ~8 cups

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed (Bastes, Brines & Sauces Excerpts).

Ingredients

  • 5 cups water
  • 1/4 cup sea or kosher salt
  • 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • 2 lemons, halved
  • 3 bay leaves (preferably fresh)
  • 8 garlic cloves, crushed and peeled
  • 2 tablespoons fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 tablespoon black peppercorns
  • 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes

Instructions

  1. Combine all ingredients in a large saucepan; bring to a boil over high heat.
  2. Transfer to a container and let cool; refrigerate overnight before using.
