Fried Shallot Loaf

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed.

Ingredients

  • 5 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon garlic salt
  • 2½ pounds shallots (about 20 shallots), sliced ⅛ inch thick and separated into rings

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oil to 350°F in a deep fryer (see Note ). Meanwhile, combine the flour and garlic salt in a large bowl and mix well. Put the shallots in a medium bowl and pour the milk and eggs over them, stirring to combine.
  2. Gently lift out the shallot rings a handful at a time, with some of the milk and egg clinging to them, and toss with the seasoned flour; the shallots will be a bit sticky and clumpy. Put the fryer basket into the fryer, drop in all the floured shallots in an even layer, and immediately put a second fryer basket on top to compress the shallots and submerge them in the oil. Fry until golden brown, 3 to 4 minutes. Carefully remove the shallots from the basket and drain briefly on paper towels, then transfer to a rack and season with salt. Serve immediately.
