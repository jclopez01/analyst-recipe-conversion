Polenta with Mascarpone and Rosemary

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed.

Ingredients

  • 6 cups milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed and peeled
  • 2 tablespoons fresh rosemary leaves
  • 2½ cups instant polenta
  • 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 1 cup freshly grated Parmesan
  • ½ cup mascarpone
  • 2 tablespoons fresh marjoram leaves
  • 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil

Instructions

  1. Combine the milk, cream, garlic, rosemary, and salt and pepper to taste in a 4-quart pot and bring just to a boil. Reduce the heat to a lazy simmer and add the polenta in a slow, steady stream, whisking constantly. Cook, stirring frequently, until the polenta is the consistency of thick porridge and smooth.
  2. Remove from the heat, add the butter, Parmesan, and mascarpone, and whisk in, slowly at first and then increasing the pace, until fully incorporated. Taste and adjust the seasoning. The polenta can be served immediately or kept warm for up to 30 minutes.
  3. To serve, transfer the polenta to a platter or serving bowl, sprinkle with the marjoram and lemon zest, and drizzle with the olive oil.
