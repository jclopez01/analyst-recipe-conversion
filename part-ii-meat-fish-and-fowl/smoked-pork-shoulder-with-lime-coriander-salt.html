Smoked Pork Shoulder with Lime Coriander Salt

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed.

Ingredients

  • One 7- to 8-pound bone-in pork butt
  • Basic Brine ( page 238 ) or 8 cups Very Basic Brine ( page 238 )
  • ¼ cup Four Seasons Blend ( page 7 )

Instructions

  1. Put the pork in a large bowl or other container and add the brine. Cover and refrigerate for 24 hours.
  2. Preheat a smoker to 275°F.
  3. Remove the pork from the brine (discard the brine) and, while it is still moist, season it all over with the seasoning blend, working it in with your hands.
  4. Put the pork fat side up on the smoker grate and smoke for 2 hours. Transfer half the baste to a bowl and set aside for finishing.
  5. Baste the pork, using the herb brush, and continue to smoke, basting every hour, until the internal temperature registers 195°F on an instant-read thermometer, about 6 hours longer.
  6. Transfer the cooked shoulder to a bowl, cover tightly with plastic wrap, and allow to rest for 30 minutes.
  7. To serve, using two forks, pull the pork into chunks. Toss with the reserved baste and finish with a sprinkling of the lime coriander salt.
