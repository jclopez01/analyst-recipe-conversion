Clinched-and-Planked Fish Steaks

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed.

Ingredients

  • Six 8- to 10-ounce fish steaks (such as salmon, halibut, tilefish, cod, or monkfish), at least 1½ inches thick
  • 1½ tablespoons Four Seasons Blend ( page 7 )
  • 2 cups baste of your choice (pages 230 – 34 ; reserve the acid component to add later)
  • About ¼ cup extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped fresh flat-leaf parsley
  • 2 tablespoons thinly sliced (on the bias) scallions
  • 1 tablespoon finely chopped chile pepper

Instructions

  1. Prepare a “mature and level” coal bed (see page 90 ), with a clean thin grate or rack set over it; the fire should be very hot.
  2. Season the fish all over with the seasoning blend, then lightly moisten your hands with water and work the seasonings into the fish. Allow to stand for 5 minutes to develop a “meat paste” (see page 8 ).
  3. Using the herb brush, moisten the fish steaks on both sides with the fat component of the baste. Put the fish in a grill basket, set it on the grill grate, and cook for 1 minute. Turn the basket over, lightly baste the fish, and cook for 1 minute, then repeat two more times, basting the fish each time the basket is flipped. Remove the basket, set it on a platter, open it, and baste the fish.
  4. Add the acid to the fat baste. Remove the grill grate. Baste the fish and arrange 2 steaks, side by side, on each plank. Put the planks on the coal bed with some coals on the exposed corners of the planks and then cover with a grill lid, a large metal bowl, or a domed lid. Cook and smoke for 5 minutes, or until the fish is just opaque throughout.
  5. Meanwhile, spread the oil on a platter and scatter the parsley, scallions, and chile over it.
  6. To serve, arrange the fish steaks on the platter, sprinkle with the salt, and squeeze the juice of the lemons over them.
