Clinched-and-Planked Game Steaks

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed.

Ingredients

  • Eight 8- to 10-ounce boneless game steaks (venison, elk, antelope, etc.), 1½ to 2 inches thick
  • ¼ cup Four Seasons Blend ( page 7 )
  • 2 cups baste of your choice (pages 230 – 34 ; reserve the acid component to add later)

Instructions

  1. Allow the game to come to room temperature.
  2. Prepare a “mature and level” coal bed (see page 90 ), with a clean thin grate or rack set over it if desired; the fire should be very hot.
  3. Season the game steaks on both sides with the seasoning blend, then lightly moisten your hands with water and work the seasonings into the meat. Allow to stand for 5 minutes to develop a “meat paste” (see page 8 ).
  4. Using the herb brush, moisten the steaks on all sides with the fat portion of the baste. Put the steaks on the grill grate or directly on the coals and cook, without moving, for 1 minute. Turn over, baste lightly, and cook for 1 minute, then repeat two more times, basting each time.
  5. Transfer the steaks to a platter, baste generously, and flip over. Allow to rest for 5 minutes.
  6. Add the acid to the fat baste. Remove the grill grate, if you used it. Baste the steaks and place 2 steaks side by side on each plank. Put the planks on the coal bed with some coals on the exposed corners of the planks and then cover with a grill lid, a large metal bowl, or a domed lid. Cook and smoke until the internal temperature registers 115° to 120°F on an instant-read thermometer, about 5 minutes.
  7. Meanwhile, pour the board dressing onto a cutting board (or mix it directly on the board). Finely chop the tip of the herb brush and mix the herbs into the dressing.
  8. Transfer the steaks to the cutting board and allow to rest for 3 minutes.
  9. To serve, slice the steaks ½ inch thick, turning each slice in the dressing to coat, and arrange on plates. Finish with a sprinkling of the salt.
