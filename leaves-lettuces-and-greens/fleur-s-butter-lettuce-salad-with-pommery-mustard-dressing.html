Fleur’s Butter Lettuce Salad with Pommery Mustard Dressing

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons Pommery mustard
  • ¼ cup champagne vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped shallots
  • ¼ cup mild extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon sea or kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped fresh chives
  • 2 tablespoons fresh dill leaves

Instructions

  1. Serve with roast chicken or stronger-tasting (oily) fish, such as salmon, mackerel, or bluefish.
  2. For the vinaigrette, whisk all the ingredients together in a small bowl.
  3. Arrange the tomatoes on a large rimmed plate and season with the salt and pepper. Sprinkle with the chives. Pour the vinaigrette over the tomatoes and let macerate for 10 minutes.
  4. Arrange the tomatoes on a large platter, leaving the vinaigrette on the plate.
  5. Put the lettuce and dill in a large bowl and drizzle with the vinaigrette, tossing gently to coat. Arrange the lettuce on top of the tomatoes and serve.
