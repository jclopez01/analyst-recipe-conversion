Baby Beet Greens and Mâche with Balsamic and Shaved Pecorino

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed.

Ingredients

  • ¼ cup champagne vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 tablespoon finely chopped shallot
  • ½ cup mild extra virgin olive oil
  • 5 cups baby beet greens
  • 5 cups mâche
  • 1 cup quartered breakfast radishes
  • ½ cup thinly sliced red onion (cut the onion in half before slicing)
  • 2 tablespoons aged balsamic vinegar
  • ¼ cup aged pecorino shavings (use a vegetable peeler)

Instructions

  1. For the vinaigrette, whisk all the ingredients together in a small bowl.
  2. Combine the beet greens, mâche, radishes, and red onion in a large salad bowl and toss with just enough vinaigrette to coat. Drizzle with the balsamic, scatter the pecorino shavings over the top, and serve.
