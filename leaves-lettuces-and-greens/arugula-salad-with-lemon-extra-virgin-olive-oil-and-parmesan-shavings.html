Arugula Salad with Lemon, Extra Virgin Olive Oil, and Parmesan Shavings

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed.

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon white wine vinegar
  • ¼ teaspoon granulated sugar
  • ¼ cup extra virgin olive oil
  • 10 cups arugula leaves
  • ¼ cup fresh flat-leaf parsley leaves
  • ¼ cup fresh dill leaves
  • ¼ cup ½-inch batons fresh chives
  • ¼ cup Parmesan shavings (use a vegetable peeler)

Instructions

  1. For the vinaigrette, whisk all the ingredients together in a small bowl. Set aside.
  2. Using a sharp serrated or other knife, cut off the top and bottom of each lemon to expose the flesh. Stand each lemon up on the cutting board and carefully cut away the skin and bitter white pith in strips, working from top to bottom and following the natural curve of the fruit. Trim away any remaining pith.
  3. To remove the lemon segments (suprêmes), work over a bowl and cut down along the membranes on either side of each section to release it, letting the sections drop into the bowl as you go. Cut each segment crosswise in half.
  4. Combine the arugula, parsley, and dill in a large salad bowl. Add the lemon segments, then toss gently with just enough vinaigrette to coat. Scatter the chive batons and Parmesan shavings over the top and serve.
