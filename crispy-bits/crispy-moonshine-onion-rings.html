Crispy Moonshine Onion Rings

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed.

Ingredients

  • 8 cups peanut oil
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons sea or kosher salt, plus more for sprinkling
  • 2 teaspoons freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 cup Original Moonshine clear corn whiskey or vodka
  • 2 cups cornstarch
  • 2 teaspoons sea or kosher salt
  • 2 teaspoons freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 teaspoons cayenne pepper
  • About 4 cups panko crumbs

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil to 350°F in an 8-quart pot. Meanwhile, put the onions in a large bowl and pour the milk over them to moisten them; drain.
  2. Put the flour in a large sealable plastic bag, add the salt and pepper, and shake to mix. Working in batches, add the drained onion slices to the flour, seal the bag, and shake vigorously to coat the slices, then spread on a baking sheet.
  3. For the batter, whip the egg whites to soft peaks in a large bowl. Fold in the moonshine. Sift the cornstarch, salt, black pepper, and cayenne over the egg whites and fold in gently.
  4. Spread the panko crumbs evenly on a baking sheet. Line another baking sheet with paper towels.
  5. Working in batches, add the onion rings to the batter, then, one at a time, toss onto the panko crumbs and flip over to coat with crumbs; repeat until you have filled the baking sheet with a generously spaced layer of onions.
  6. One by one, drop the coated onions into the hot oil, without crowding, and cook until golden brown, 3 to 4 minutes. Remove with a spider or slotted spoon, transfer to the lined baking sheet to drain briefly, and sprinkle with salt, then transfer to a mesh cooling rack (this will prevent the onion rings from becoming soggy). Repeat with the remaining onion rings and serve.
