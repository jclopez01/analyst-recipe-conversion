Clinched Beef Tenderloin

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed.

Ingredients

  • Three 1¼-pound center-cut beef tenderloin roasts
  • ¼ cup Four Seasons Blend ( page 7 )
  • 1 cup Butter Baste ( page 234 )

Instructions

  1. Allow the beef to come to room temperature, approximately 1 hour if straight from the refrigerator.
  2. Prepare a “mature and level” coal bed (see page 90 ), with a clean thin grate or rack set over it if you like; the fire should be very hot.
  3. Season the beef all over with the seasoning blend, then lightly moisten your hands with water and work the seasonings into the meat. Allow to stand for 5 minutes to develop a “meat paste” (see page 8 ).
  4. Fan or blow-dry excess ash from the coal fire (see photos, page 90 ).
  5. Using the herb brush, brush the beef lightly all over with the butter baste. Put the roasts on the grill grate or directly on the coals and cook, without moving them, for 1 minute. Roll the roasts a quarter turn and cook for another minute, then repeat until you’ve cooked them for 1 minute each on all four “sides.”
  6. Transfer the roasts to a platter and brush lightly all over with the butter baste. Put back on the grill grate or coals and repeat the cooking routine above, cooking for 1 minute each on all four “sides.”
  7. Transfer the roasts to the platter, brush lightly with the baste, and let rest for at least 5 minutes, and up to 30 minutes.
  8. Put the meat back on the coal bed, or on the rack, and cook, rolling the roasts a quarter turn every minute, until the internal temperature registers 115°F on an instant-read thermometer, about 5 minutes.
  9. Meanwhile, pour the board dressing onto a cutting board (or mix it directly on the board). Finely chop the tip of the herb brush and mix the herbs into the dressing.
  10. Transfer the beef to the cutting board and roll the roasts in the dressing to coat. Allow to rest for 5 to 10 minutes.
  11. To serve, slice the meat ¼ inch thick, turning each slice in the dressing to coat, and arrange on plates. Pour some of the board juices over each serving and finish with a sprinkling of the salt.
