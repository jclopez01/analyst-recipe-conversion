Clinched Boneless Pork Chops

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed.

Ingredients

  • Six 8- to 10-ounce boneless pork loin chops, at least 2 inches thick
  • Basic Brine ( page 238 ) or 8 cups Very Basic Brine ( page 238 )
  • 3 tablespoons Four Seasons Blend ( page 7 )
  • ½ cup Butter Baste ( page 234 )

Instructions

  1. Put the pork chops in a heavy-duty plastic bag or a medium bowl and add the brine. Seal the bag or cover the bowl and refrigerate for at least 12 hours, and up to 16 hours.
  2. Drain the pork chops (discard the brine) and pat dry with paper towels. Allow to come to room temperature, approximately 1 hour.
  3. Prepare a “mature and level” coal bed (see page 90 ), with a clean thin grate or rack set over it if you like; the fire should be very hot.
  4. Season the pork chops on both sides with the seasoning blend, then lightly moisten your hands with water and work the seasonings into the meat. Allow to stand for 5 minutes to develop a “meat paste” (see page 8 ).
  5. Fan or blow-dry excess ash from the coal fire (see photos, page 90 ).
  6. Using the herb brush, brush the pork chops lightly all over with the butter baste. Put the pork chops on the grill grate or directly on the coals and cook, without moving them, for 2 minutes. Turn the chops, baste lightly, and cook for 2 minutes, then repeat two more times, basting the chops each time they are flipped.
  7. Lean the pork chops up against one another, fat side down, and cook for 1 minute, or until the internal temperature registers 135°F on an instant-read thermometer.
  8. Meanwhile, pour the board dressing onto a cutting board (or mix it directly on the board). Finely chop the tip of the herb brush and mix the herbs into the dressing.
  9. Transfer the pork chops to the cutting board and turn them in the dressing to coat. Allow to rest for 5 minutes.
  10. To serve, arrange the pork chops on plates and pour some of the board juices over each one.
