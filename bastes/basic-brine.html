Basic Brine

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed.

Ingredients

  • Makes approximately 8 cups
  • 8 cups water
  • ¼ cup sea or kosher salt
  • 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • 8 garlic cloves, crushed and peeled
  • 2 tablespoons fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 tablespoon black peppercorns
  • 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes

Instructions

  1. Combine all the ingredients in a large saucepan and bring to a boil over high heat. Transfer to a bowl or other container and allow to cool, then refrigerate overnight before using.
