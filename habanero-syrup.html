Habanero Syrup

Makes ~1½ cups

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed (Bastes, Brines & Sauces Excerpts).

Ingredients

  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 habanero pepper, seeded and finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed and peeled
  • 1/2 cup finely chopped scallions
  • Juice of 1 lime

Instructions

  1. Combine sugar, water, habanero, and garlic in a small saucepan; bring to a simmer, stirring to dissolve sugar.
  2. Remove from heat; stir in scallions and lime juice.
  3. Let stand at least 1 hour to develop flavor. Refrigerate up to 1 week.
