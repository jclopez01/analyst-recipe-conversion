BBQ Sauce

Makes ~4 cups

By Adam Perry Lang. Excerpted for personal use from Charred & Scruffed (Bastes, Brines & Sauces Excerpts).

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 5 garlic cloves, crushed and peeled
  • 2 teaspoons garlic salt
  • 1 tablespoon chili powder
  • 1 cup packed light brown sugar
  • 2 cups water
  • 2 cups ketchup
  • 1/2 cup unsulfured blackstrap molasses
  • 3/4 cup cider vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1/2 cup yellow mustard
  • 1/2 cup red pepper jelly
  • 1/4 teaspoon liquid smoke (or to taste)

Instructions

  1. Combine oil and garlic in a medium pot over low heat; cook, stirring occasionally, until garlic is lightly golden, 2–3 minutes.
  2. Stir in garlic salt, chili powder, and brown sugar; cook 1 minute.
  3. Add water, ketchup, molasses, vinegar, Worcestershire, mustard, pepper jelly, and liquid smoke; bring to a boil.
  4. Reduce heat and simmer gently, stirring frequently to prevent scorching, until reduced to about 4 cups, 20–30 minutes.
  5. Cool; refrigerate up to 1 week.
